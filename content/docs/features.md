SKIES connects to your organization's community service. What you see depends on what your organization has enabled.

## Posts

- Latest news and announcements, with pinned posts at the top
- Rich text, images, YouTube videos and attachments
- Unread indicators, badges and **Mark all as read**
- Likes and comments, including liking comments
- Bookmarks for posts you want to keep
- **Keywords**: get an alert when new content mentions a word you choose, see matches tagged in the feed, and filter to them (tagging and the filter are coming in 0.10)

## Activities

- Upcoming events with relative dates such as *Today* and *Tomorrow*
- Sign up with guests and answers to the organizer's question, or join the waitlist when it's full
- Participant list with capacity, attendance and dietary notes
- Add an activity to your calendar and get a reminder the day before or on the day
- **Calendar sync** keeps a SKIES calendar up to date with the activities you join
- **Notify me when a spot opens** on a full activity
- **Activity History**: the activities you've joined, with counts by year, month and category *(coming in 0.10)*
- A Live Activity on the iOS Lock Screen for the next activity you joined
- Birthday banners for colleagues celebrating today

## People

- Profiles with contact details, job, department and an "about me"
- Call, email or start a Microsoft Teams chat from a profile
- Your own QR contact card, with the fields you choose to share

## Search

- One search across posts, activities and people
- Accent-insensitive: "kovacs" finds "Kovács"
- Works offline on cached content and remembers recent searches on the device
- On iOS, posts and activities you opened also appear in Spotlight

## Offline and sync

- Posts, activities and profiles are cached in an encrypted database
- **Keep Bookmarks Offline** saves the text, images and attachments of your bookmarks on the device
- Background checks for new content can notify you (the system decides when they run)
- Notifications let you mark an item as read without opening the app, and on iOS they can show its image *(coming in 0.10)*
- **iCloud Sync** keeps settings, bookmarks, read state and, from 0.10, activity history in step across your Apple devices
- **Handoff**: start reading a post or activity on one Apple device and continue on another *(coming in 0.10)*

## Summaries

On iOS 26 devices with Apple Intelligence, SKIES can summarize long posts and activities on the device. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can edit the prompt it uses.

## Widgets and shortcuts

- **Next Activity**, **Birthdays** and **Unread Posts** widgets for the iOS and Android home screen
- Long-press the app icon to jump to posts, activities, search or your QR code
- Keyboard shortcuts on iPhone and iPad with a hardware keyboard; hold ⌘ to see them

## Make it yours

- Light, dark or system theme and a choice of accent colors
- Wallpaper colors on Android
- Alternate app icons, including seasonal ones
- In-app or system browser for links
- A Safari extension on iOS that opens portal links in SKIES
- Guided setup on first launch, which you can replay from Settings

## Security

- Sign-in through your organization's Microsoft account, with private browser sessions
- **App Lock** with a passcode or biometrics
- Encrypted cache and tokens kept in the system keychain or keystore

## Platforms

| Platform | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone and iPad | iOS and iPadOS 18 or later |
| Android phones, tablets and foldables | Android 14 or later |

On iPad, tablets, foldables and dual-screen phones, SKIES uses a sidebar with list and detail side by side, and you can resize the panes.

## Languages

English, German, Hungarian, Norwegian Bokmål, Albanian, Serbian, Swedish, Tamil and Ukrainian.
