Plans change with feedback and with what the service's API allows. Have an idea? [Get in touch](/docs/contact/).

## Planned

- **1.0 release.** Stabilize the new iOS and Android apps for a 1.0 release.
- **Open source.** Publish the source code under GPL-3.0.
- **Handoff.** Continue reading a post on another Apple device.
- **Keyword highlights.** Mark posts as important when they contain words you choose, and filter by them.

## Ideas

- **Apple Watch** app with upcoming activities and quick sign-up
- **Creating and editing** posts and activities, if the API allows it
- **Personal history**: past events you attended, with simple stats
- **Activity suggestions** based on what you've joined before
- **Multiple sites**: switch between more than one organization in the app
