Version 0.10 adds ways to keep up without opening the app, and a few to look back at what you've done.

## What's new

- **Activity history.** See the activities you've joined, with counts for this year, by month and by category. With iCloud Sync on, your history follows you across your Apple devices.
- **Keyword highlights.** Posts and activities that mention one of your keywords are tagged in the feed, and a new **Keywords** filter shows only those. You can turn highlighting off on the Keywords page.
- **Keyword alerts.** Get notified when new content mentions a word you choose.
- **Handoff.** Start reading a post or activity on one Apple device and pick it up on another.
- **Richer notifications.** On iOS, new-content notifications can show the post's image. On both platforms you can mark an item as read straight from the notification.
- **Android widgets.** *Next Activity*, *Birthdays* and *Unread Posts* are now on the Android home screen too.
- **Unread Posts widget.** A new widget lists what you haven't read, and lets you mark posts as read from the home screen.
- **Live Activities.** Your next activity appears on the iOS Lock Screen as it approaches.
- **Calendar sync.** Keep a SKIES calendar up to date with the activities you join.
- **Notify me when a spot opens.** Watch a full activity and get an alert if someone drops out.
- **Offline bookmarks.** Keep the text, images and attachments of your bookmarks on the device.
- **Spotlight.** Posts and activities you opened appear in iOS search.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** on iPhone and iPad with a hardware keyboard. Hold ⌘ to see them.
- **Safari extension** that opens portal links in SKIES.

## What's improved

- **Widgets show something useful when you're caught up.** With nothing unread, *Unread Posts* lists the latest posts. With no joined activity ahead, *Next Activity* shows what's coming up next.
- **Bookmarks** have an **All** tab next to Posts and Activities.
- **Keywords** can be edited even when notifications are off.
- **Sheets on dual-screen phones** keep their buttons clear of the side rail.

## Good to know

- iCloud Sync uses a new format in this version. Your devices sync with each other again once they are all on 0.10.
- Notification images are iOS-only for now. Android notifications get the action button without the image.
