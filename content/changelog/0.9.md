SKIES has been rebuilt from scratch as a single app for iPhone, iPad and Android. The 0.9 series, from 0.9.0 to 0.9.8, brings it to every platform and lays the groundwork for 1.0.

## What's new

- **Android.** SKIES is now on Google Play for Android 14 and later, with wallpaper colors and native haptics.
- **Guided setup.** First launch walks you through language, theme, feed style, notifications and iCloud Sync. You can replay it from Settings → About.
- **Widgets.** *Next Activity* and *Birthdays* widgets for the iOS home screen.
- **Adaptive layouts.** A sidebar with list and detail side by side on iPad, Android tablets, foldables and dual-screen phones. The panes are resizable.
- **Nine languages.** English, German, Hungarian, Norwegian Bokmål, Albanian, Serbian, Swedish, Tamil and Ukrainian.
- **Comments.** Write and reply to comments, and like them.
- **Likes.** Like posts and activities.
- **Calendar and reminders.** Add activities to your calendar and get a reminder before activities you've joined.
- **Background updates.** Get notified when a background check finds new posts or activities.
- **Recent searches** are remembered on your device.

## What's improved

- **Security.** Cached content now lives in an encrypted database, and sign-in uses OpenID Connect with PKCE.
- **Summaries** include event details and use an editable prompt.
- **Rich text.** Posts render formatting, links and embedded videos more faithfully.
- **Feeds** show placeholders while loading and group unread items.
- **Accessibility.** Better screen-reader labels throughout.
- **Times and dates** follow your device's 12/24-hour setting and locale.
