## What it does

- **Posts** News and announcements, with pinned posts, unread tracking, likes and comments.
- **Activities** Join events with guests and answers, see who's coming, and add them to your calendar.
- **People** Search colleagues, open their profiles, start a Teams chat or share your own QR contact card.
- **Works offline** Content is cached in an encrypted database, so the feed and search still work without a connection.
- **On-device summaries** Apple Intelligence can summarize long posts without anything leaving your device.
- **Made for every screen** Widgets on iOS, adaptive layouts for iPad, tablets, foldables and dual-screen phones.

## Private by design

SKIES talks only to your organization's service and its sign-in provider. There are no analytics, ads or tracking, and no SKIES server stores your data. Read the [privacy policy](/docs/privacy-policy/).
